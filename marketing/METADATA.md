## Description (4000 max)

    SpeedRead is a cozy reader with a speed-reading superpower.
    
    Tap any word in any book and it expands into a single-word RSVP stream — one word at a time, centered exactly where your eye naturally lands. The red ORP letter cuts eye movement to zero. You read faster because you stop moving your eyes.
    
    300 WPM is comfortable. 500 WPM is books-in-a-day. 700 WPM is for when you have a flight to catch.
    
    WHAT'S INSIDE
    • Tappable word reader with three cozy themes — parchment, sepia, night
    • Tap-to-RSVP — drop into speed mode mid-sentence, jump back exactly where you stopped
    • Adjustable WPM 60–900 with Optimal Recognition Point (ORP) alignment
    • Bookmarks, highlights, last-read tracking — all local
    • Adjustable font size and line spacing
    • Reading-time estimates at your current speed
    • Apple Watch companion for hands-free reading on the go
    
    FORMATS
    Import your own PDF, EPUB, RTF and TXT files from anywhere — Files, iCloud, AirDrop, email. The library ships with four classics so you can start reading immediately: The Yellow Wallpaper, Metamorphosis, and A Christmas Carol (in both PDF and EPUB).
    
    PRIVACY FIRST
    Nothing leaves your device. No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your library, your speed, your business.
    
    ABOUT RSVP
    Rapid Serial Visual Presentation is a research-backed reading technique that presents text one word at a time at the same on-screen position. By eliminating saccadic eye movement, readers can comprehend text 2–4× faster than traditional reading. SpeedRead pairs RSVP with a classic paragraph reader so you can switch modes whenever you want — speed when you need to cover ground, paragraph when you want to savor it.
    
    The library is yours. The pace is yours. Sit back and read.

## What's New (4000 max)

    Chapters. Jump around a book from its chapter list without losing your place.

    Your library server keeps up. On a server that supports it, your reading position, highlights, notes and bookmarks follow the book.

    iPhone and Apple Watch share one place. Start on one and the other shows "Reading on...". Tap to take over.

    A scrub you can see. Drag in RSVP and the words move with your finger, glide after a flick and tick as they pass.

    Long tokens read cleanly. Web addresses stay whole and hold longer, and very long words break into hyphenated pieces.

    PDFs you import on iOS 27 no longer pick up stray junk words, and a scanned PDF with no text now says so instead of opening as an empty book.

    And a new app icon.
